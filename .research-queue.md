### [DL-001] No idempotency guard on delivery — duplicate Discord posts
- **File**: `src/deliver/webhook.ts:283-331`
- **Category**: data-integrity
- **Description**: deliver() doesn't check delivery_status before posting. Same report can be delivered twice (e.g., catch-up race).
