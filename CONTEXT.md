# Context Glossary

## New Junkyard

New Junkyard is the rehauled successor to Podders v2. It keeps Podders' 24/7 collector, entity memory, reports, dashboard, and ops foundation, but changes the product center from crypto/Indonesian market reports to a chokepoint thesis book powered by a self-revising discovery engine.

## AI Bottlenecks-style Book

The user-facing product shape for New Junkyard: a dashboard/book with watchlist, chokepoints/themes, catalysts, brief, ask-AI, and regime/history views. The book shows what names and chokepoints matter; the regime engine explains why the map exists and when it changes.

## Edge Signal Miner

The discovery framework inside New Junkyard. It watches niche expert/source clusters, converts evidence into typed artifacts, proposes chokepoint candidates and regime changes, tests them through gates, and preserves accepted/rejected/superseded transitions.

## Regime

A versioned representation map for a domain: the artifact types, relation types, operations, and verifiers currently used to understand a niche. Example: `AI buildout = GPUs + fabs` versus `AI buildout = GPUs + packaging + HBM + power + cooling + construction`.

## Typed Artifact

A fact-like object with a declared type, payload, provenance, confidence, and regime context. Examples include Observation, Relation, Candidate, Thesis, StressTest, GateResult, RejectedAlternative, TransportMapping, and ResidualAnalysis.

## Observation

A typed, provenance-backed fact extracted from an item, summary, report, or external verification source. Observations are raw material for candidates, theses, gates, and anomaly pressure.

## Candidate

An entity, ticker, bottleneck, event, or relationship worth watching under a regime. A candidate is not the core state machine; it is a view over the typed artifact graph and regime state.

## Thesis

A concise claim about why a candidate or chokepoint matters, what evidence supports it, what would falsify it, and which regime makes it meaningful.

## Gate Result

A recorded evaluation of a candidate, thesis, or regime proposal against a named verifier. Gate results carry outcome, score, evidence, rationale, and provenance.

## Regime Proposal

A proposed change to the current map: new artifact type, relation type, verifier, or schema frame. It must be scoped, stress-tested, gated, transported, and residual-checked before acceptance.

## Transport

The act of mapping old artifacts into a proposed new regime before claiming discovery. Transport prevents the system from calling a simple relabeling a discovery.

## Residual Content

The new explanatory or predictive content that remains after old artifacts are transported into the new regime. If there is no residual content, the change is search/relabeling rather than discovery.

## Regime Transition

An append-only lifecycle event that accepts, rejects, or supersedes a regime proposal. Rejected and superseded alternatives remain queryable for audit and later revival.
